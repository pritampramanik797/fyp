* 45nm CMOS XOR Gate Netlist
.include 45nm_HP.pm

* Power Supply - 45nm HP typically operates at 1.0V to 1.1V
Vdd VDD 0 1.0V
Vss VSS 0 0V

* Inputs (Faster switching for 45nm)
VinA A 0 PULSE(0 1.0 0 50p 50p 5n 10n)
VinB B 0 PULSE(0 1.0 0 50p 50p 10n 20n)

* --- Inverter for A ---
M1 A_bar A VDD VDD pmos W=180n L=45n
M2 A_bar A VSS VSS nmos W=90n L=45n

* --- Inverter for B ---
M3 B_bar B VDD VDD pmos W=180n L=45n
M4 B_bar B VSS VSS nmos W=90n L=45n

* --- XOR Logic Core (12T) ---
M5 N1 A VDD VDD pmos W=180n L=45n
M6 OUT B N1 VDD pmos W=180n L=45n
M7 N2 A_bar VDD VDD pmos W=180n L=45n
M8 OUT B_bar N2 VDD pmos W=180n L=45n

M9 OUT A N3 VSS nmos W=90n L=45n
M10 N3 B_bar VSS VSS nmos W=90n L=45n
M11 OUT A_bar N4 VSS nmos W=90n L=45n
M12 N4 B VSS VSS nmos W=90n L=45n

* Load Capacitance (Crucial for 45nm simulation)
CLoad OUT 0 5fF

* --- Simulation Settings ---
.tran 10p 40n

* --- Power Measurement ---
.measure tran avg_pwr avg @Vdd[i]*V(Vdd)

.control
run
plot V(A) V(B) V(OUT)
.endc
.end
